\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We showed that a bit-exact port turns WCET analysis of a data-dependent industrial kernel into
a tractable, largely automated pipeline: structural bounds enforced and machine-checked in the
port, worst inputs found by a deterministic counter-guided search (with an exact saturation
certificate for the dominant term), and cross-language equal-work measurement requiring no
instrumentation of the production C++ sources. On every adversarial fixture the Rust port meets
or beats the C++ engine's observed maximum while eliminating its per-point heap-allocation
hazard.
