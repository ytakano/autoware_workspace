\section{Threats to Validity}
\label{sec:threats}

% Be brutally honest here; it is what makes the rest credible.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Measurement environment.} Current host numbers come from a development
    container with the \texttt{powersave} CPU governor and warm caches; they support
    \emph{relative} (cross-engine, same-machine) claims only. \todo{replace with
    performance-governor / isolated-core / cold-cache / co-runner data.}
  \item \textbf{EVT rigor.} 100 samples per fixture, block maxima of 10, moment-fit Gumbel:
    adequate for comparison, not certification. No exponentiality test, no confidence
    intervals, single machine. \todo{1000+ samples, PoT/MLE fit, CIs, repeated runs.}
  \item \textbf{Search completeness.} The hill-climb explores a parameterized generator, not
    the full input space; the saturation argument covers the kernel-evaluation term exactly,
    but the kd-traversal term has no matching analytic maximum, so a worse traversal geometry
    may exist. The structural $O(N_{\mathrm{leaves}})$ bound still caps it.
  \item \textbf{Real-data representativity and the divergence finding.} The real-data
    reference (Sec.~\ref{sec:eval-realdata}) is one route, one vehicle, and a
    \emph{degraded-prior} guess track (offline NDT-odometry, not the production EKF); its
    iteration statistics over-represent nominal cruising and its margins are specific to that
    drive. It also surfaced a finding the synthetic suites never did: iteration
    counts diverged by ${\pm}1$ on ${\sim}2\%$ of real frames. Root-causing identified and
    fixed two port-fidelity gaps invisible to translation-only fixtures --- the C++ engine
    transforms its \emph{initial} pass by the guess matrix (not the Euler rebuild), and pcl's
    SSE transform kernel is right-associated with the translation innermost --- reducing the
    divergence to $28$ of \num{22416} frames ($0.12\%$). The residual is irreducible without
    matching glibc's eigensolver and \texttt{f64} transcendentals bit-for-bit: sub-f32-ULP
    differences in the internal \texttt{f64} trajectory (eigensolver ${\le}2{\times}10^{-15}$
    on the inverse covariances; \texttt{libm}-vs-glibc \texttt{exp}) surface only on
    knife-edge frames. ``Bit-exact'' therefore means bit-exact on the tested domain: equal work
    is \emph{certified per frame} by the replay assertion, and the $0.13\%$ is flagged rather
    than silently compared.
  \item \textbf{Input realizability and caller-side assumptions.} The engine-level fixtures
    are API-legal but not reachable through the production preprocessing pipeline (duplicate
    source points, sub-downsample map clusters, overlapping tiles); the bound they support is
    the \emph{untrusted-input} tier, deliberately conservative. The deployment tier is measured
    separately (Sec.~\ref{sec:eval-twotier}), but its contracts --- tile disjointness and
    alignment, map/scan downsampling, the $P \le 1500$ cap --- are properties of the map and
    sensing pipelines that the engine does \emph{not} verify; a violated contract silently
    moves the system back to the engine-level tier. On the iteration axis the tier's time bound
    (\emph{legal-worst}) pins $N_{\mathrm{iter}} = 30$ via a non-production $\epsilon$; the
    cap itself is legally reachable (the production-parameterized \emph{legal-osc} witness,
    Sec.~\ref{sec:impl-legal}), but no single legal input in our search family achieves the
    $K = 8$ and 30-iteration worst cases \emph{simultaneously} --- the tier bound therefore
    combines the two axes conservatively rather than exhibiting one witness for both. The affine $\mathrm{WCET}(P)$ law is
    validated over $P \in [\psweepPmin{}, \psweepPmax{}]$ on the host
    (Sec.~\ref{sec:eval-psweep}); beyond that range, working sets exceeding the last-level
    cache could bend the slope upward, and the engine's zero-allocation guarantee is tied to
    the pre-reserved capacity (exceeding it costs a one-time buffer growth). \maxnn{}
    truncation deviates from C++'s unbounded neighbor set only beyond ${\ge}3$ adversarially
    crowded overlapping tiles (Sec.~\ref{sec:implementation}: the per-tile geometric cap is
    27); the derivation additionally assumes the search radius stays coupled to the voxel size
    --- decoupling them would require re-deriving the cap.
  \item \textbf{Cross-ISA transfer.} ISA-independence of the Rust floating-point stream is by
    construction (pure-software \texttt{libm}) and verified via counter equality, but the C++
    comparison itself is inherently x86/Linux-bound (pcl/ROS dependency).
  \item \textbf{Absolute WCET: what is proven and what is observed.} None of the reported
    times is a certified hard bound, but the layers carry different epistemic strength, and the
    distinction is the point of the decomposition. The \emph{work} bound of
    Eq.~\eqref{eq:decomp} is proven --- its terms are enforced by construction and
    machine-checked, so no input can exceed it --- and for the kernel-evaluation term a frozen
    input provably \emph{achieves} the maximum (the saturation certificate; the kd-traversal
    term has no matching certificate, so a worse traversal geometry may exist within its
    structural cap). Every \emph{time} figure, by contrast, is an observation: warm caches,
    one container, no interference, and a microarchitecture (out-of-order x86) for which no
    sound static timing model exists --- on this hardware class a true WCET is practically
    unobtainable by any method, and the Gumbel extrapolation inherits MBPTA's representativity
    caveats. The counter decomposition thus \emph{factorizes} WCET into a proven part (work)
    and an empirical part (time per unit of work); only the latter is unproven. On a
    timing-predictable target --- precisely the \texttt{no\_std} kernel deployment of
    Sec.~\ref{sec:intro} --- a defensible per-operation cost bound would promote
    $\text{work bound} \times \text{unit-cost bound}$ to an analytical WCET. Separately, the
    structural-hazard removals (allocation-, recursion-, panic-freedom) are constructive
    properties, not measurements: they are what make a finite bound \emph{possible} at all ---
    an engine that calls the allocator inside its align loop forfeits the premise of a finite
    WCET under fragmentation. The deliverable is therefore ``the port does not regress WCET on
    any adversarial fixture, removes the structural hazards, and carries a proven work
    envelope'' --- not a proven absolute time bound.
\end{itemize}
