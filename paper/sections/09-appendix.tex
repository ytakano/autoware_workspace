\subsection{Cross-ISA discrepancy diagnosis}
\label{app:crossisa-debug}
The initial AArch64 mismatch on \emph{search-01} reproduced under QEMU and hardware.
Bisection found a stored expectation predating the PCL \texttt{f32} fidelity fix, not an ISA effect;
re-baselining made host, emulator, and hardware counters bit-identical.
